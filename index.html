Where every meal feels like a feast

In Hindi, rasoi means kitchen, and mahal means palace. Our idea is simple: royal flavour should not cost a royal price.

We grind our masalas in-house, simmer gravies slowly and bake breads only when you order them. Come with family, bring friends, or take a hot meal home.
